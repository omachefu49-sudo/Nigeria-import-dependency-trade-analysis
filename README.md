# Nigeria's Import Dependency & Trade Concentration Analysis

**Project Status:** 🟡 Planned

An independent economic and trade data analytics project investigating Nigeria's import dependency, international trade patterns and exposure to external supply and market risks.

## 1. Problem Statement

Dependence on imported goods exposes economies to foreign exchange pressures, international price fluctuations and external supply disruptions.

However, understanding these vulnerabilities requires more than examining total import expenditure. It requires identifying critical import commodities, major trading partners and areas of excessive trade concentration.

This project aims to evaluate Nigeria's import dependency and identify potential vulnerabilities within its international trade structure.

## 2. Research Objectives

- Examine Nigeria's import and export trends.
- Identify major imported commodities and product categories.
- Assess dependence on selected imported products.
- Analyse concentration among major trading partners.
- Investigate trade balance patterns.
- Examine potential foreign exchange and external supply vulnerabilities.
- Compare Nigeria's trade structure across selected economies.
